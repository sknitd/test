    From farm to roast table

    The people behind the beans

    This week’s roast starts with Lucía Vargas at Finca El Roble in Huila, Colombia.

    Lucía and her team pick ripe cherries by hand and keep each harvest lot separate through washing and drying. We buy directly from Lucía and taste samples alongside her notes on when each lot was picked and how it dried.

    Those conversations help us choose what to roast each week. We picked this lot for its crisp red-apple acidity and the panela sweetness that comes through as the cup cools.
